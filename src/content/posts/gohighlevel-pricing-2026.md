## Starter — $97/month

Starter is the entry ticket, and it's the most popular plan for **solo operators and small businesses**. You get the complete core platform:

- Full CRM with pipelines and contact management
- Drag-and-drop funnels, websites, and landing pages
- Email and SMS marketing automation (usage-billed)
- Appointment booking calendar with reminders
- Reputation and review management
- Social media planner

Starter includes up to **3 sub-accounts** — enough for your own agency account plus one or two client accounts. The moment you land a third or fourth client, you'll want Unlimited.

**Verdict:** pick Starter if you're running your own marketing or managing a single client. At $97/month it replaces a CRM, funnel builder, email tool, and scheduler that would otherwise cost $300+ combined.

## Unlimited — $297/month (the agency default)

Unlimited is where GoHighLevel becomes an *agency platform*. The $200/month jump over Starter buys four things that matter:

1. **Unlimited sub-accounts** — onboard every client with no per-seat tax
2. **White-label desktop app** — client-facing work carries your branding
3. **Full API access** — build custom integrations and workflows
4. **Advanced reporting & branded client portal** — deliver professional reporting

If you manage even three clients, Unlimited pays for itself: at $297/month spread across 3 clients, your platform cost is under $100 per client while replacing thousands in tool spend.

**Verdict:** this is the plan most agencies should choose. If you're asking "Starter or Unlimited?", the honest answer is: count your clients. More than two? Go Unlimited.

## SaaS Pro — $497/month

SaaS Pro adds **SaaS Mode** — the ability to repackage GoHighLevel as your own branded software, set your own pricing tiers, and bill clients automatically through Stripe.

What's added on top of Unlimited:

- SaaS Mode with snap-on pricing tiers
- Rebilling for SMS, email, and AI usage (charge your markup, keep the difference)
- White-label mobile app (available as an add-on)
- Priority support SLA

With SaaS Mode, client software becomes **monthly recurring revenue you own**. Sell "your platform" at $297–$997/month per client while paying GoHighLevel $497/month flat — the margin model is the entire point.

**Verdict:** choose SaaS Pro when you're ready to sell software, not services. It's overkill for pure service agencies — and the single highest-ROI plan for productized agencies.

## The costs nobody quotes: usage fees

GoHighLevel's subscription covers the software. **Usage-based channels are billed separately** at pass-through carrier rates:

- Email sends
- SMS segments
- Phone minutes & voicemail drops
- AI message responses

Most agencies budget an extra **$20–$150/month** depending on client volume. On SaaS Pro you can re-bill this usage at a markup, turning it into a profit line instead of a cost.
