"Should I use GoHighLevel or ClickFunnels?" is the most common question we get from agency owners. Both build funnels. Both promise to grow your business. But they're built for **fundamentally different business models** — and picking wrong costs thousands per year.

Here's the honest 2026 comparison.

## The one-line answer

- **ClickFunnels** is a dedicated *sales funnel builder* — great if funnels are your whole business.
- **GoHighLevel** is an *all-in-one agency platform* — funnels included, plus CRM, SMS, calling, scheduling, reputation management, and white-label SaaS.

If you sell funnels to clients or run an agency, the short version is: GoHighLevel consolidates far more. Let's dig into why.

## Feature-by-feature breakdown

### Funnels & landing pages — tie
Both have mature, proven funnel builders with templates, A/B testing, and checkout pages. ClickFunnels has more funnel-specific polish; GoHighLevel's builder covers funnels, full websites, and blogs.

### CRM & pipeline management — GoHighLevel wins
ClickFunnels added a light CRM, but GoHighLevel was *built* around one: multi-stage pipelines, smart lists, conversation tracking across email/SMS/Facebook/Instagram, and workflow triggers on any CRM event. Agencies live in pipelines — this matters.

### SMS, calling & voicemail — GoHighLevel wins clearly
This is the biggest gap. GoHighLevel has **native two-way SMS, outbound calling, and voicemail drops** inside workflows. ClickFunnels relies on third-party integrations for SMS. If you run follow-up campaigns for clients, native SMS isn't a nice-to-have — it's the product.

### Scheduling & reputation — GoHighLevel wins
GoHighLevel includes an appointment calendar with automated reminders, plus review-request automation for Google and Facebook. ClickFunnels includes neither.

### Courses & memberships — tie
Both deliver membership content and courses well. Kajabi still leads for pure course businesses, but either platform handles digital products.

### White-label & SaaS mode — GoHighLevel wins by definition
Only GoHighLevel lets you **rebrand the platform as your own software** (SaaS Pro, $497/mo) and bill clients automatically. For productized agencies this is a business model, not a feature.

## Who should pick ClickFunnels?

- Solo entrepreneurs whose core need is *building sales funnels*
- Marketers who want the largest funnel template ecosystem and community
- Businesses already deep in the ClickFunnels ecosystem (Follow-Up Funnels, Backpack)

## Who should pick GoHighLevel?

- **Agencies managing client marketing** — unlimited sub-accounts on the $297 plan
- Businesses that want **SMS + email + CRM + scheduling in one bill**
- Anyone wanting to **resell software** with white-label SaaS Mode
- Operators consolidating 4–6 subscriptions into one platform

## The cost of the stack nobody compares

ClickFunnels at $97/month plus a CRM ($50–150), an SMS platform ($50+), a scheduler ($15–30), and a review tool ($30–100) means your real stack costs **$250–400+/month** — more than GoHighLevel Unlimited, which includes all of it.

That's the comparison most "ClickFunnels vs GoHighLevel" articles skip.

## Verdict

For funnel-focused solo marketers, ClickFunnels remains a solid pick. For **agencies and client-service businesses**, GoHighLevel wins nearly every dimension that matters: CRM depth, native SMS/voice, scheduling, reputation management, and the ability to white-label the whole thing.

Most of our readers end up on the [$297/month Unlimited plan](/#pricing) — unlimited client accounts is the feature agencies can't outgrow.
