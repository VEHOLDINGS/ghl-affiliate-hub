"GoHighLevel or Kajabi?" is a question we hear from two very different audiences: **agency owners** who want an all-in-one client platform, and **course creators** who want to sell knowledge products. The right answer depends almost entirely on which one you are.

Here's the honest 2026 breakdown.

## The one-line answer

- **Kajabi** is a premium **course and knowledge-business platform** — best if your product is education.
- **GoHighLevel** is an **agency and marketing platform** — best if you sell marketing services (or software) to clients.

They overlap in funnels, email, and checkout — but diverge sharply on CRM, SMS, and white-labeling.

## Feature-by-feature

### Courses & memberships — Kajabi wins
This is Kajabi's home turf: polished course player, student progress tracking, communities, coaching products, and podcast hosting. GoHighLevel delivers memberships and courses well enough for most use cases — but Kajabi's product experience is purpose-built.

### CRM & pipelines — GoHighLevel wins clearly
Kajabi has contact management; GoHighLevel has a full **CRM with multi-stage pipelines**, conversation tracking (email, SMS, social DMs), and workflow triggers on any CRM event. If you manage leads for clients, there's no contest.

### SMS, calling & voicemail — GoHighLevel wins
GoHighLevel includes **native two-way SMS, calling, and voicemail drops** inside automations. Kajabi is email-first — SMS relies on integrations. For local-business marketing, SMS is often the channel that converts.

### Funnels, email & checkout — tie
Both build landing pages, funnels, and email sequences competently. Kajabi's templates skew toward course launches; GoHighLevel's toward lead capture and client campaigns.

### White-label & SaaS reselling — GoHighLevel wins by definition
Only GoHighLevel's SaaS Pro plan ($497/mo) lets you **resell the platform as your own branded software**. Kajabi has nothing comparable.

## Who should pick Kajabi?

- Course creators and educators whose **product is the course**
- Coaches packaging memberships, communities, and digital products
- Creators who value polished student experience over CRM depth

## Who should pick GoHighLevel?

- **Agencies and freelancers** managing marketing for clients
- Businesses that need **SMS + CRM + scheduling** in one subscription
- Operators who want to **resell software** (SaaS Mode)
- Anyone consolidating a stack of 4–6 tools into one bill

## The cost math nobody runs

A course creator with 2,000 contacts on Kajabi Basic ($179/mo) is paying for product polish. An agency on GoHighLevel Unlimited ($297/mo) is replacing a CRM ($100+), SMS platform ($50+), funnel builder ($100+), scheduler ($20+), and review tool ($30+) — **$300+/month in separate tools**.

Compare stacks, not sticker prices.

## Verdict

**Selling courses? Kajabi.** **Selling marketing services or software? GoHighLevel.** There are edge cases — course creators who also run heavy SMS marketing often add GoHighLevel alongside Kajabi — but for most buyers, the business model decides.

If GoHighLevel fits your model, the [$297/month Unlimited plan](/#pricing) is where most agencies land.

## FAQ

### Is Kajabi or GoHighLevel better for course creators?
Kajabi. Its course player, student experience, and product tooling are more mature. GoHighLevel works for simple membership content but isn't course-first.

### Can GoHighLevel replace Kajabi entirely?
For basic memberships and drip content, yes. For cohorts, student communities, and premium course experiences, Kajabi retains an edge.

### Which has better email marketing?
Both are capable. Kajabi's email is tuned for launch sequences; GoHighLevel's for behavior-triggered multi-channel workflows. For SMS-heavy follow-up, GoHighLevel wins.

### Do both offer free trials?
Yes — 14 days each at the time of writing. Confirm current trial terms on each vendor's site.
