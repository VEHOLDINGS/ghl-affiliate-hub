You've started your GoHighLevel trial — now what? The platform does a *lot*, which is exactly why new users freeze. This guide takes you from empty account to a working agency stack in a single weekend, in the order that actually works.

## Before you start (15 minutes)

- Start your [14-day free trial](/#pricing) (Starter is fine to learn on; Unlimited if you're already managing clients)
- Grab your brand assets: logo, colors, domain, email signature
- List your tools to replace: CRM, funnel builder, email/SMS, scheduler, review tool

## Saturday morning: foundations (2–3 hours)

### 1. Set up your agency profile
Complete Settings → Company: name, logo, colors, email sending domain. If you're on Unlimited or SaaS Pro, configure white-label settings now — your client-facing work should carry your brand from day one.

### 2. Create sub-accounts
A sub-account = one business location (yours or a client's). **Starter includes up to 3; Unlimited is unlimited.** Create your own first — it's your sandbox.

### 3. Import contacts
Bring in your CSV. Tag aggressively (`source:`, `service:`, `status:`) — tags are the backbone of every automation you'll build later. Junk in, junk out: clean the list before importing.

## Saturday afternoon: the revenue tools (3–4 hours)

### 4. Build your first funnel
Pick one offer. Build three pages: **landing → application/checkout → thank you**. Use a template and customize it — perfection here is procrastination. Connect a form or survey to capture contact details into the CRM.

### 5. Turn on the two automations that pay for the plan
These are the workflows most agencies deploy first because they produce results *fast*:

**Missed-call text-back** — when a call is missed, an SMS fires within seconds ("Sorry we missed you — how can we help?"). For local businesses, this alone recovers leads that would otherwise die.

**Review request** — after a job completes (pipeline stage change or form trigger), send an SMS asking for a Google review with a direct link. Reviews compound local rankings and conversion for years.

### 6. Connect your calendar
Set up the appointment calendar with your availability, reminder emails/SMS, and no-show follow-up. Drop the booking link on your funnel's thank-you page.

## Sunday morning: polish & launch (2–3 hours)

### 7. Email/SMS campaigns
Write a simple 3-message welcome sequence (deliver → give value → offer next step). Keep SMS under 160 characters and always include an opt-out.

### 8. Reputation dashboard
Connect Google Business Profile/Facebook, verify review requests flow, and set up alerts for new reviews. This becomes the report clients love.

### 9. Reporting & pipeline stages
Customize your pipeline stages to match your actual sales process (e.g., New Lead → Contacted → Booked → Proposal → Won/Lost). Dashboards are mostly useful once there's data — don't over-engineer on day one.

## Sunday afternoon: the client-ready checklist

- [ ] Funnel live on your domain, forms tested end-to-end
- [ ] Missed-call text-back firing (test with a real call)
- [ ] Review request sending (test with a dummy contact)
- [ ] Calendar booking confirmed with reminders
- [ ] Welcome sequence scheduled
- [ ] White-label branding applied (Unlimited / SaaS Pro)

That's a functioning marketing stack — built in about 10 focused hours.

## What to do in week 2

The trial covers 14 days; use the second week to (1) run real traffic or outreach through the funnel, (2) refine automations based on what actually happens, and (3) decide your plan — most agencies land on [Unlimited ($297/mo)](/#pricing) for unlimited client accounts.

## Common beginner mistakes

- **Building 12 funnels instead of one that works.** One offer, one funnel, real traffic.
- **Skipping tags.** Untagged contacts are unautomatable.
- **Ignoring usage costs.** Email/SMS are billed by usage — set budgets in Settings (see our [pricing guide](/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-2026)).
- **Over-customizing white-label before selling anything.** Branding matters, but revenue first.

## FAQ

### How long does GoHighLevel setup take?
A working solo stack takes a weekend (8–12 focused hours). Agency setups with client sub-accounts and multiple funnels typically take 1–2 weeks.

### Do I need technical skills?
No — the builders are drag-and-drop. The steepest learning curve is *strategy* (what to automate), not the software.

### Should I start on Starter or Unlimited?
If you manage (or plan to manage) more than two client accounts, start on Unlimited. Otherwise Starter is a fine, cheap classroom.

### Can I migrate funnels from another tool?
There's no universal importer — plan to rebuild live funnels manually. Most teams rebuild only their revenue pages and start fresh otherwise.
