SaaS Mode is the feature that makes GoHighLevel unique among marketing platforms: instead of *using* the software, you **sell it as your own**. Clients log into a platform branded as yours, pay you monthly, and never know GoHighLevel exists.

Here's how it works, what it costs, and whether it's worth it in 2026.

## What is SaaS Mode?

SaaS Mode (included in the **SaaS Pro plan, $497/month**) lets you:

1. **White-label the entire platform** — your logo, your domain, your brand
2. **Create your own pricing tiers** — you set what clients pay
3. **Bill clients automatically** — Stripe-powered subscriptions flow to you
4. **Rebill usage** — mark up SMS, email, phone, and AI usage and keep the margin

The client experience is a standalone SaaS product. Yours.

## The economics: how the math works

Your fixed cost: **$497/month** (SaaS Pro) plus pass-through usage.

Your revenue: whatever you charge. Typical agency SaaS tiers run $297–$997/month per client. A common structure:

| Your tier | Your price | Clients | Monthly revenue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $297/mo | 5 | $1,485 |
| Professional | $497/mo | 10 | $4,970 |
| Agency | $997/mo | 20 | $19,940 |

At 10 clients on a $497 tier, you're grossing **$4,970/month** against a $497 platform cost — before usage rebilling margin.

### Usage rebilling: the hidden margin

On SaaS Pro you can charge clients **your** rates for SMS, email, and AI. Buy at carrier cost, sell at your markup — many agencies add 20–40% margin here without the client ever noticing a separate vendor bill.

## Who is SaaS Mode for?

- **Productized agencies** wanting recurring software revenue (not just service retainers)
- Agencies serving **local niches** (dental, fitness, real estate) where a branded "all-in-one platform" pitch converts
- Operators tired of being judged on hours — software revenue scales without headcount

It's **overkill** if you only manage 1–2 clients or purely sell services. Unlimited ($297/mo) covers those cases.

## Getting started in 5 steps

1. **Upgrade to SaaS Pro** — SaaS Mode unlocks on the $497/month plan
2. **Configure your brand** — logo, colors, domain, email sending domain
3. **Design 2–3 pricing tiers** — keep it simple; most agencies offer Starter / Pro / Agency
4. **Connect Stripe** — subscriptions, trials, and usage billing flow automatically
5. **Migrate or onboard clients** — spin up sub-accounts on your branded platform

The full setup usually takes a weekend; the sales process is the longer part.

## The honest caveats

- **You own support.** Clients call *you*, not GoHighLevel — budget time for onboarding and tickets
- **$497/month is real money** until you have a few clients signed
- **Positioning matters.** "My software" sells better than "my services" — but only if you deliver outcomes on it
- **Migration friction** — moving existing clients from other tools takes effort (but sticksier retention)

## Is SaaS Mode worth it?

If you have (or can realistically get) **5+ clients** paying for a branded marketing platform, the math is hard to beat: one fixed cost, unlimited client accounts, recurring revenue you own. If you're just starting out, begin with [Unlimited](/#pricing) and upgrade when client #3 or #4 signs — the platform cost difference pays for itself.

## FAQ

### What plan includes SaaS Mode?
SaaS Pro at $497/month — it includes everything in Unlimited plus SaaS Mode, rebilling, and Stripe subscription billing.

### Can clients tell it's GoHighLevel underneath?
No — the platform is white-labeled: your brand on the desktop app, client portal, and (optionally) mobile app.

### Do I need my own Stripe account?
Yes. Payments flow through your Stripe account on your pricing — GoHighLevel never touches your client revenue.

### Can I still use SaaS Mode for my own agency?
Absolutely — many operators run their agency inside the same platform they resell.
